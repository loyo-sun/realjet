<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

A fabricated panel provides a surface for covers, controls, access or attachment. Its openings must line up with fitted parts after any edge forming and finishing, while the visible surfaces meet the specified appearance.

For this product, the process is applied to **supply equipment panels with a defined dark exterior finish**. The particular review priorities are visible surfaces, edge coverage and masked interfaces; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review the interface layout and visible faces.**
2. **Cut openings and the outer profile.**
3. **Form edge returns and attachment details.**
4. **Complete finishing and hardware installation.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Provide the panel layout and identify fitted controls, connectors or adjacent covers.
