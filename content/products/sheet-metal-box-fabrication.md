<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

A fabricated box uses formed or joined panels to create an internal volume with a defined cover or opening. A useful specification describes both the external envelope and the space left after flanges, seams and hardware are installed.

For this product, the process is applied to **build a repeatable box body from cut and formed panels**. The particular review priorities are corner joints, bottom geometry and cover fit; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Define internal and external dimensions.**
2. **Cut and form the body and cover.**
3. **Join corners and prepare mounting positions.**
4. **Apply the finish and install agreed fittings.**
5. **Check closure, envelope and accessible space.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify whether the requested supply is a shell, a body-and-cover set or a complete box assembly.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about sheet metal box fabrication</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify corner joints, bottom geometry and cover fit, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Identify whether the requested supply is a shell, a body-and-cover set or a complete box assembly.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with steel, stainless steel or aluminum to drawing. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Confirm joint style and the required assembled condition, including any seal or hardware. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
