Order the metal tray that supports your GPU baseboard assembly and locates it inside the server chassis. REALJET manufactures the formed tray and drawing-defined reinforcement to your part and assembly drawings.

<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

The tray has to carry the specified assembly while allowing it to enter the chassis and approach its connectors correctly. Show the board support heights, chassis fixing points and insertion direction together. A correct external size alone will not establish that fit.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Prepare the tray blank

Review bend-adjacent holes, board supports and reinforcement locations before blank preparation. Keep the insertion direction visible in the work drawing.

### Form without losing support geometry

Choose a bend order that leaves access to the inner returns. Locate reinforcement from the tray datum and review joining order to limit changes in the support plane.

### Finish locating features

Review support-face or locating-hole machining after reinforcement is attached where the drawing requires it. Keep finish build-up away from specified contact features.

<h2 id="inspection">Define what the first article must demonstrate</h2>

| Feature | Proposed check | Record to agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support height and tray twist | Locate the tray at its assembly datums and measure the individual support heights and opposite corners. Confirm the selected measuring setup can reach the inner features. | Support-height readings and the datum/support setup. |
| Insertion and fixing | Fit the nominated chassis or gauge and check travel, fixing alignment and connector approach clearance without using the connector to force alignment. | Fit checklist identifying the mating part or gauge revision. |
| Deflection under module load | Review a test using the specified mass, support span and load positions; measure movement at defined points and after unloading. | Load arrangement, readings and acceptance limits; test provision to be confirmed. |

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Protect the support faces and keep formed edges from carrying packing loads. Identify trays by drawing revision so similar server variants remain separate during assembly. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### When should support heights be checked?

Check the formed tray before reinforcement hides the features, then verify the finished support geometry after joining and surface treatment. The agreed report identifies the measurement datums and individual support positions.

### Can the tray fit different GPU server configurations?

Each tray is made to its approved board and chassis interfaces. Provide the configuration drawing or revision for each variant rather than assuming one tray fits every server.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
