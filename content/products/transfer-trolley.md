## Supported transfer of long concrete piles

A spun pile transfer trolley supports long concrete piles as they move between production or finishing stations. Its frame and support arrangement are selected for the product mass, geometry and condition during transfer.

The trolley supports piles during movement between defined stations. Support spacing and contact surfaces should suit the pile geometry and condition at the time of transfer, while the travel arrangement must fit the plant circulation plan.

## Configuration and integration

- Rated load and pile length range
- Support spacing and contact arrangement
- Track or travel-route geometry
- Drive, towing and positioning interfaces

## Information for equipment selection

Pile sizes and mass, number of piles per transfer, support requirements, route layout and transfer frequency.

## Selection questions

### Is this the same as a mould-carrying trolley?

This page covers pile transfer. A trolley intended to carry a complete steel mould has different support and load interfaces, so the two applications should be specified separately even when they share a travel route.

### What determines the pile support arrangement?

Pile length, mass, the number carried and the approved handling requirements establish the support positions and contact arrangement. The route and receiving station then determine travel and positioning requirements.
