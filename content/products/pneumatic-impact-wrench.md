## Repeated mould opening and closing work

A pneumatic impact wrench assists with repeated fastener removal and installation on split spun pile moulds. Socket fit, access along the mould flange and the workstation air supply determine the practical tool arrangement.

The wrench supports repeated fastener removal and installation at the mould assembly stations. Socket access, tool weight and available air supply affect tool selection. Where a specified final tightening value is required, the agreed fastening procedure must include a suitable verification method.

## Configuration and integration

- Mould fastener and socket dimensions
- Required working range and access length
- Air pressure and consumption
- Tool handling and fastening procedure

## Information for equipment selection

Fastener sizes, mould flange layout, tightening requirements, available air supply and operating frequency.

## Selection questions

### Does an impact wrench verify final bolt tightening?

The tool assists fastening, but its use alone does not verify a specified final tightening value. Follow the agreed mould-fastening procedure and its verification method where a controlled final value is required.

### Which information is needed to select the wrench?

Provide fastener and socket sizes, access length, required working range and available air pressure and flow. The frequency of mould opening and closing also helps define tool handling and workstation requirements.
