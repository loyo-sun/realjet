## Connect batching with casting

The system transfers concrete from the agreed supply point to the required mould or workstation. Routing, capacity and discharge control are planned around the production sequence and building conditions.

## Equipment interfaces

The proposal considers batching-plant discharge, travel route, crane conflicts, cleaning access, power supply and the receiving mould. The final solution may use different conveying and distribution arrangements depending on the project.

## Proposal information

Provide concrete mix characteristics, batch volume, required delivery rate, travel distance, workshop layout and discharge locations. Testing and acceptance conditions should be agreed for the selected concrete and production cycle.
