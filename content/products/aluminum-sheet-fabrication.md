<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Aluminum fabrication uses cutting, bending and joining to produce lightweight equipment parts. Alloy and temper affect forming, welding and finishing, so they must be specified rather than inferred from the appearance of a sample.

For this product, the process is applied to **supply lightweight brackets, panels and formed housings for equipment builders**. The particular review priorities are alloy, temper, stiffness and fastening interfaces; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.
