## Production scenarios and design basis

Floor and roof slab moulds are used in building-component factories producing repeated horizontal structural elements. The equipment concept is coordinated with slab dimensions, edge profiles, openings, cast-in items, reinforcement or prestressing where applicable, required surface orientation and the planned lifting and stacking method.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Level steel casting surfaces sized for the approved slab family
- Repositionable edge forms and inserts where controlled dimensional variation is required
- Interfaces for openings, service penetrations and cast-in hardware shown on approved drawings
- Compatibility with the selected concrete distribution, vibration and curing process
- Reference features for repeat setup and routine dimensional inspection

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Slab drawings, size and thickness schedule, edge details, opening and embedded-item plans, reinforcement or prestressing method, finish requirements, target cycle and handling arrangement.
