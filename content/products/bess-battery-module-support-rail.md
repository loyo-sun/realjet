## Battery Module Support Rails to your design

Source the formed rails that support and guide battery modules into your rack. REALJET makes each rail to the section profile, fixing pattern and paired-rail arrangement in your assembly drawing.

<h2 id="supply">Choose the part and delivery condition</h2>

| Your order | Information that sets the scope |

| Individual support rail | Section drawing, length and fixing pattern |

Supply covers the formed metal rail and agreed attachments. It does not include a telescopic slide, battery module or electrical assembly unless explicitly quoted.

<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

Rail spacing and height matter as a pair, not just as individual parts. Define the installed span, straightness and clearance for module insertion. Include stops or restraint points where your rack design uses them.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Review the rail section

Review bend radii and mounting-hole positions against the long section. Identify the left/right parts, installed support span and module entry direction.

### Plan long-part forming

Review tool access, rail handling and the location of any joining features. Check the section along its length rather than accepting a single cross-section.

### Keep matched pairs identified

Apply the specified finish and maintain pair identity. Review treatment of module sliding/contact edges and mounting seats.

| Feature | Proposed check | Record to agree |

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Keep paired rails together and label handed variants. Support long rails during packing so stacking and straps do not change their straightness. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Why inspect the rails as a pair?

Two individually acceptable rails can still leave the installed support heights or spacing incorrect. The paired check should use the cabinet attachment datums and the module insertion envelope.

### Can the rails be supplied as a matched pair?

Yes. Provide the pair assembly drawing, installed spacing and identification requirements. Agree whether the pair is checked with a module or an approved gauge.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
