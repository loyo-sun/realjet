<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

Module insertion should reach the intended support and fixing points without the carrier obstructing the connector or removal tools. Show the insertion direction, connector approach and service clearance on the assembly drawing. EV charging and AI power applications require separate module interfaces.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Review entry and connector zones

Locate carrier supports and fixings relative to the insertion path. Resolve apertures close to bends before the blank is prepared.

### Form the carrier

Plan the bend order to preserve access to inner supports. Add only the connections or reinforcement present in the component drawing.

### Finish entry and locating edges

Review burr removal, finish allowance and hardware installation at module seats. Inspect locating features after finish where treatment affects fit.

<h2 id="inspection">Define what the first article must demonstrate</h2>

| Feature | Proposed check | Record to agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module envelope and supports | Measure slot dimensions, support height and fixing pattern from the carrier datums. | Support/slot readings and datum setup. |
| Insertion and fixing | Use the specified module/dummy to check the insertion path, seating and fastener access; keep connector mating separate from structural alignment. | Insertion/fixing checklist and dummy revision. |

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Protect entry edges and attachment tabs. Identify module variants by part number so similar carriers are not mixed during cabinet assembly. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Can a loose power module alone prove the carrier fits the cabinet?

It can check some module interfaces, but not the full installed access. The first-article plan should represent the cabinet or shelf attachment and service space as well as the module.

### Can the carrier be made for our power module?

Yes. Send the module envelope, support and fixing details with the cabinet or shelf interface. Identify any connector zones that the metalwork must keep clear.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
