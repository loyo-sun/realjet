## Marine caisson production applications

Precast concrete caisson moulds are reviewed for large hollow marine structures used in harbour, quay, breakwater and coastal works. Engineering starts from the approved external geometry, internal-cell arrangement, construction joints, casting stages, reinforcement and embedded items.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Modular outer form panels configured around the approved caisson dimensions
- Internal forms reviewed for cell geometry, withdrawal direction and working access
- Stiffeners, ties, supports and platform interfaces developed for the agreed casting sequence
- Concrete placement, vibration and inspection access coordinated with reinforcement and embedded items
- Opening, stripping, lifting and form-repositioning methods reviewed against the production-yard plan

Final structural checks, panel arrangement, materials, tolerances and operating method remain subject to the approved design basis and project documents.

## Required project inputs

Use the enquiry Message to outline the caisson dimensions, internal-cell layout, casting stages, target production programme, lifting or handling plan, yard conditions and whether approved drawings are available. Detailed drawings are requested through an appropriate follow-up channel.
