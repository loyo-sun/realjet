<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review frame layout, load basis and mounting references.**
2. **Prepare sections, plates or formed members.**
3. **Fit and join parts using the agreed sequence.**
4. **Machine or finish designated interfaces.**
5. **Check diagonals, mounting planes and assembly fit.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Provide the approved structural arrangement and load cases where required; rated performance must be established for that design.
