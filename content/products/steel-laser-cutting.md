<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Laser cutting follows a digital profile through metal sheet or plate to make outlines, holes and slots. The selected stock, feature size and edge specification determine whether a part can be used as cut or needs secondary processing.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review profiles, units, material and drawing revisions.**
2. **Plan nesting and cut-path features.**
3. **Cut profiles with the selected process settings.**
4. **Remove burrs and complete agreed secondary operations.**
5. **Inspect critical contours and package flat parts.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Mark precision interfaces explicitly; a nominal hole in a cutting file does not define the required final fit.
