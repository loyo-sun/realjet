## CDU Chassis Frames to your design

Source the metal chassis that supports the pump, heat exchanger and enclosure panels in your coolant distribution unit (CDU). REALJET makes the frame to your cooling-equipment layout and assembly drawing.

<h2 id="supply">Choose the part and delivery condition</h2>

| Your order | Information that sets the scope |
| --- | --- |
| Chassis skeleton | Frame geometry, connections and equipment layout |
| Frame with mounting supports | Pump and heat exchanger support drawings and datums |
| Frame ready for panel fitting | Panel attachment details and required finished interfaces |

The frame order covers the agreed metal structure. Pumps, heat exchangers, pipework, coolant and controls are separate items; the chassis does not establish cooling capacity or pressure performance.

<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

Check equipment mounting positions alongside the access needed to remove a pump or heat exchanger. Show panel joints, rack or floor connections and pipe-routing space. A frame for AI server cooling and one for battery-storage cooling use their own mounting and service layouts.

| Intended installation | Equipment interface to supply |
| --- | --- |
| AI equipment | AI server liquid-cooling CDU |
| Battery storage equipment | Energy-storage coolant distribution equipment |

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Set equipment mounting datums

Review pump and heat exchanger attachment points together with the chassis base and removable panels. Resolve access to connections before forming or welding.

### Build around service space

Review the tack/joining sequence and temporary locating supports. Avoid using removable panels to pull a distorted skeleton into position.

### Finish equipment seats

Review any post-join mounting-face or hole machining. Mask specified seats and maintain the clearance allowed for final surface treatment.

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Protect the pump and heat exchanger mounting faces. Identify lifting and restraint points on the assembly drawing so packing supports do not load service-panel connections. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Can the frame be checked before pumps and pipework arrive?

A nominated dummy or fixture can check the mechanical mounting envelope and service path if it represents the approved equipment interfaces. It cannot validate coolant circulation, cooling duty or pressure integrity.

### Can you make the frame for an AI data-center CDU?

Yes, we review the metal frame from your equipment and component drawings. Identify the pump, heat exchanger, enclosure and rack or floor interfaces to be included.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
