<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Stamping uses tooling to cut or form repeat component features. Tooling design, material feed and production quantity influence whether stamping is appropriate compared with a cutting-and-bending route.

For this product, the process is applied to **combine cutouts, embossing and formed details in repeat-order OEM parts**. The particular review priorities are forming sequence, burr direction and assembled fit; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review part geometry and annual quantity.**
2. **Define tooling scope and material specification.**
3. **Produce and inspect initial samples.**
4. **Agree sample corrections and release the production revision.**
5. **Run and inspect batches against the approved requirements.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. State expected batch and repeat quantities so tooling investment can be considered alongside piece cost.

Review coating, plating or a bare-metal finish against the specified material and environment. Identify appearance zones, threads, grounding contacts and fitted surfaces before defining masking and finishing requirements.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom metal stampings</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify forming sequence, burr direction and assembled fit, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. State expected batch and repeat quantities so tooling investment can be considered alongside piece cost.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified formable metal stock. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Define which dimensions are measured before and after forming and establish die maintenance requirements for repeat orders. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
