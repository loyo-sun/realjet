<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Locate the frame interfaces

Review member preparation, weld access and the rail/base datum scheme. Define which purchased sections enter the frame and how they are identified.

### Review welding and restraint

Plan tack-up, connection order and restraint around the rail interfaces. Measure the frame after release from the fixture so restraint does not conceal free-state distortion.

### Machine only specified interfaces

Review access for post-weld face or hole machining and the lifting arrangement. Finish protection and rail locating faces are resolved before treatment.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Is a diagonal check enough to accept the rack frame?

No. Diagonals help describe squareness, but rail location, straightness and base interfaces also need checks. Measurements should identify the support condition and whether the frame is free of assembly-fixture restraint.

### Can the frame be made for our rail spacing?

Yes. Provide the rail interface drawing, datum scheme and required rack envelope. Include the mating rail specification or checking fixture where available.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
