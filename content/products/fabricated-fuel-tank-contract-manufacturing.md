## Drawing-based fuel tank fabrication

REALJET provides custom fabricated fuel tanks to your drawings for OEM and contract manufacturing orders. The scope combines tank welding and finishing around the approved part and assembly requirements.

Machinery fuel tanks often combine a constrained installation envelope with defined capacity, mounting, filling, venting and outlet requirements. Realjet provides contract manufacturing for fabricated tanks when customers supply authorised drawings, material specifications and validation criteria.

The representative tank shown was manufactured under recorded authorisation. Its formed shell, welded seams, lifting features and connection details illustrate the production scope available for industrial and construction machinery components. Final suitability for a fuel type or machine is governed by the customer's design, applicable requirements and approved test plan.

## Production and quality scope

The process can include plate cutting, bending, fabrication of internal or external details, fixture assembly, controlled welding, port preparation, leak testing, surface treatment and final inspection. Machining or thread finishing is applied where the drawing requires accurate connection interfaces.

Important control items include:

- correct material, thickness and component traceability;
- overall dimensions and mounting-point position;
- weld continuity and agreed leak-test results;
- filler, vent, drain and outlet interface conformity;
- internal cleanliness before closure and shipment;
- specified paint or powder-coating condition.

## Application and enquiry basis

This made-to-drawing service can support construction equipment, lifting machinery, mobile plant, industrial vehicles and stationary power equipment. It is intended for OEM or project supply rather than retail replacement sales. Prototype quantities, scheduled batches and repeat production can be evaluated against the required tooling and inspection level.

To request a manufacturing review, send the complete drawing pack, bill of materials, fluid specification, required tests, coating system, packaging expectations and forecast quantity.
