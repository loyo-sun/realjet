<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Review component mounting and clearances

Relate component seats, panel joints and service openings to one chassis datum. Identify electrical-clearance and bonding features from the approved equipment drawing.

### Form and join the enclosure parts

Review bend access and hardware-installation access before closing the shell. Confirm whether mounting features need finishing after joining.

### Apply the specified finish

Review coating build-up and masked contacts, then inspect the assembly interfaces in the delivered condition. Electrical performance is not established by the metal fabrication route.

<h2 id="inspection">Define what the first article must demonstrate</h2>

| Feature | Proposed check | Record to agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Component seats | Measure hole/seat locations and heights from the chassis datums. Use nominated component dummies for a separate interference check. | Mounting measurements and dummy revisions. |
| Panels and openings | Check joint alignment and service access after joining and finish. | Panel-gap/opening results and assembly observations. |
| Contact and clearance provisions | Measure drawing-defined metal clearances and masked areas. Electrical continuity, insulation or safety tests need a separate confirmed route. | Dimensional/finish record distinguished from electrical test results. |

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Specify whether chassis parts arrive loose or joined. Protect panel edges, machined mounting features and the finish, and keep revision identification with each set. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Does checking the chassis clearances validate the PCS electrically?

No. Dimensional inspection can verify the specified metalwork features. Complete electrical safety and performance require the defined equipment assembly and test scope.

### Can you manufacture only the PCS shell or a mounting panel?

Yes. Send the part drawing with an assembly view showing the interfaces. The quotation can cover individual metal parts or a defined chassis structure.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
