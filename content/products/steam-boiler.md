## Steam supply sized around the curing schedule

It provides steam to the curing pits or chambers. Boiler capacity is selected from the curing volume, cycle time and site utility conditions.

Steam demand depends on the curing volume, heat losses and how many pits or chambers operate at the same time. Boiler selection should be coordinated with the steam distribution system, water treatment and the utilities available at the site.

## Configuration and integration

- Peak and average steam demand
- Steam conditions at the curing-system interface
- Site fuel or energy supply
- Feedwater, condensate and installation scope

## Information for equipment selection

Curing schedules, simultaneous chamber load, required steam conditions, fuel availability and installation location.

Realjet can include the steam boiler in a coordinated spun pile equipment package or review it for an existing production line. Equipment dimensions, capacity, controls and supply boundaries are confirmed against the project requirements before an equipment proposal is finalized.
