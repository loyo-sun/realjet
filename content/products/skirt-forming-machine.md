## Steel skirts for pile-end assemblies

A skirt forming machine shapes steel strip into the skirt used in a spun pile end assembly. The forming geometry is selected to match the pile diameter and the approved end-plate connection.

The skirt forms part of the pile-end assembly and must fit the specified end plate and pile geometry. Strip preparation, forming and subsequent joining operations need to be defined as separate parts of the manufacturing scope.

## Configuration and integration

- Steel strip width, thickness and grade
- Finished skirt diameter and profile
- Forming tooling and size changeover
- Interfaces with end-plate assembly

## Information for equipment selection

Pile-end drawings, strip specifications, skirt dimensions, size range and production quantity.

## Selection questions

### Is skirt forming the same as end-plate manufacturing?

No. This page covers forming the steel skirt. End-plate manufacture, hole preparation and subsequent joining or assembly are separate operations whose inclusion must be stated in the equipment package.

### Can one machine produce different skirt sizes?

A size range can be reviewed from the required diameters, strip widths, thicknesses and profiles. Tooling and changeover requirements must be checked against that range rather than assuming that all pile sizes use the same setup.
