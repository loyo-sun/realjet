## Pump Mounting Plates to your design

Order the metal mounting plate between your cooling pump and base frame. REALJET manufactures the profile, reinforcement and specified machined seating areas from your pump-foot and equipment drawings.

<h2 id="supply">Choose the part and delivery condition</h2>

| Your order | Information that sets the scope |
| --- | --- |
| Mounting plate | Plate profile, pump-foot pattern and frame attachment |
| Plate with reinforcement | Reinforcement drawing and joining requirements |

Supply is the plate and agreed reinforcement or hardware. The pump, isolators and pipework are separate unless listed in the order. Vibration isolation and pump-system design are defined by your equipment specification.

<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

Show the pump feet and base-frame interface on the same datum scheme. Identify any isolator positions and the clearance needed for fasteners and maintenance. AI cooling CDUs and battery thermal-management units may require different pumps and support arrangements.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Separate locating and clearance holes

Review the pump-foot and frame connections, seating areas and any isolator provision. Identify which holes locate the pump and which only admit fasteners.

### Join reinforcement before final seats where needed

If reinforcement is welded, review the weld sequence and the condition in which the seating plane is machined. Machining order follows the datum scheme and drawing.

### Finish faces and holes

Review milling of specified seating faces and drilling/boring of defined hole features. Protect machined contacts during the quoted surface-treatment route.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Protect machined seating areas and hole edges. Use supports that prevent handling damage to the pump interface and keep the finished faces away from packing straps. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Should the seating faces be machined before reinforcement is welded?

The review considers whether joining can change the seating plane. Where the drawing requires a finished plane after welding, the proposed route places the relevant machining and final measurement after the joining operation.

### Can you machine the pump seating faces?

Face machining and hole machining can be reviewed from your drawing. Specify the datums, finish and dimensional limits so the machining and inspection scope can be quoted.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
