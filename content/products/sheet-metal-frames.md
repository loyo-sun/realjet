<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

A fabricated frame locates equipment through an arrangement of connected members or formed sections. Alignment of the complete assembly and the condition of its mounting interfaces matter as much as the dimensions of individual pieces.

For this product, the process is applied to **use folded and joined sheet sections to locate equipment components**. The particular review priorities are section stiffness, joined corners and mounting patterns; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review frame layout, load basis and mounting references.**
2. **Prepare sections, plates or formed members.**
3. **Fit and join parts using the agreed sequence.**
4. **Machine or finish designated interfaces.**
5. **Check diagonals, mounting planes and assembly fit.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Provide the approved structural arrangement and load cases where required; rated performance must be established for that design.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for fabricated sheet metal frames can include specified steel, stainless steel or aluminum sheet. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Review coating, plating or a bare-metal finish against the specified material and environment. Identify appearance zones, threads, grounding contacts and fitted surfaces before defining masking and finishing requirements.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about fabricated sheet metal frames</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify section stiffness, joined corners and mounting patterns, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Provide the approved structural arrangement and load cases where required; rated performance must be established for that design.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified steel, stainless steel or aluminum sheet. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Check the final frame geometry and how mating panels seat against it. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
