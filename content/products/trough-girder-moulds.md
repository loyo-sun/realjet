## Production scenarios and design basis

Trough girder moulds are reviewed for rail, urban transport, bridge and other approved infrastructure applications. The mould concept follows the approved trough section, wall and bottom geometry, reinforcement, embedded items, prestressing method where applicable, casting orientation and demoulding route.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Inner, outer, bottom and end forms matched to the approved trough geometry
- Opening and withdrawal sequence reviewed against the available movement envelope
- Interfaces for reinforcement, embedded items, prestressing and lifting provisions
- Conventional, mechanical, hydraulic or movable operation selected for the agreed workflow
- Access for concrete placement, vibration, inspection, cleaning and maintenance

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Approved trough girder drawings, component schedule, section and length range, tolerance basis, reinforcement and embedded-item details, prestressing information, target output, casting sequence, yard layout and handling plan.
