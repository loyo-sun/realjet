## Adjustable formwork for project-defined girder families

Highway and viaduct projects may use a family of U-girders with related but not identical lengths, end details or cross-section dimensions. Adjustable steel U-girder formwork can reduce the need to treat every variant as a completely separate mould, provided the permitted adjustment range is defined during engineering.

Realjet reviews the component schedule to identify which surfaces remain constant, which dimensions change and where replaceable or movable mould sections are practical. The resulting system can include adjustable end forms, controlled side-form positions, interchangeable inserts and reference points for dimensional verification.

## Product features

- fabricated steel contact surfaces shaped for the required U-girder profile;
- adjustable sections defined by the approved range of component variants;
- mechanical or hydraulic movement selected for the operating sequence;
- stiffened support frames designed around concrete pressure and mould length;
- repeatable stops and locking positions for production setup;
- working access for reinforcement, embedded items, casting and inspection;
- interfaces for vibration, curing and lifting equipment.

Adjustment does not replace engineering control. Each permitted configuration should have a defined setup method, locking condition and dimensional inspection procedure. This helps prevent uncontrolled changes between production batches.

## Suitable project scenarios

Adjustable U-girder formwork is relevant to highway bridges, urban viaducts, elevated transport structures and precast yards producing several related beam types. It is especially useful when the production programme contains repeated elements with limited dimensional variation.

The proposal should be based on the full component schedule, tolerances, casting sequence, required output and available production space. Crane coverage, concrete delivery, prestressing operations, curing occupancy and finished-product handling should be reviewed together with the mould.
