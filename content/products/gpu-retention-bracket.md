<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Review the blank and bend allowance

Check hole-to-bend distance, the card contact edge and tool clearance on the dimensioned drawing. Blank preparation is included through the quoted processing route.

### Form the offset

Plan bends so the return flange remains accessible. Review material condition and bend radius for the specified offset; do not use a cosmetic edge as the inspection datum.

### Complete a small-part route

Deburr card-facing edges and apply the specified finish. Welding or milling is added only for an actual drawing feature; a simple bent bracket does not need a blanket machining operation.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Separate small brackets to protect the finish and prevent bent tabs. Keep different offsets or hole patterns labelled by part number and revision. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Does a small bracket need welding or milling?

Only if those operations are required by its features. For an ordinary formed retention bracket, the review focuses on the blank, bend offset, fixing holes and edge finish. Spring latches need a separate material and forming review.

### Can you make a bracket from our PCIe GPU layout?

Yes, the metal bracket can be reviewed from your dimensioned drawing and assembly view. Identify the card envelope, attachment points and areas that must not contact electronics.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
