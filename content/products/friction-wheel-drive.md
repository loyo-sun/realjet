## Ground-level trolley movement between stations

It drives a trolley along the planned ground transfer route. Drive force, speed and stopping points are set for the trolley load and line layout.

The drive transfers force through contact with the trolley interface. The contact arrangement, load and travel route must be reviewed together so the system can move and position the trolley within the planned production sequence.

## Configuration and integration

- Trolley contact surface and supported load
- Required driving force and travel speed
- Drive placement and station spacing
- Stopping, position sensing and control interfaces

## Information for equipment selection

Trolley drawings, loaded mass, route layout, gradients, travel speed and stopping positions.

## Supply and project coordination

Realjet can include the friction-wheel drive in a coordinated spun pile equipment package or review it for an existing production line. Equipment dimensions, capacity, controls and supply boundaries are confirmed against the project requirements before an equipment proposal is finalized.
