## Controlled prestressing before centrifugal forming

It tensions the reinforcement after the cage is placed in the mould and before spinning. Load, stroke and control steps follow the approved prestressing design.

The tensioning station must connect correctly to the mould and reinforcement anchorage. Force, elongation and the operating sequence are defined by the approved pile design; equipment selection also considers access and the method used to verify the applied load.

## Configuration and integration

- Required tensioning force and working stroke
- Mould-end connection and anchorage interface
- Load measurement and control arrangement
- Operating records and line-control interfaces

## Information for equipment selection

Prestressing design, reinforcement details, mould-end drawings, force and stroke requirements and operating sequence.

## Supply and project coordination

Realjet can include the pile tensioning machine in a coordinated spun pile equipment package or review it for an existing production line. Equipment dimensions, capacity, controls and supply boundaries are confirmed against the project requirements before an equipment proposal is finalized.
