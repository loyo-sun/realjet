## Production scenarios and design basis

T-girder moulds are developed for precast factories and project casting yards serving highway, viaduct and related bridge works. The mould arrangement starts with the approved T-section, girder length range, reinforcement and embedded-item layout, prestressing method, casting sequence and component-handling plan.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Bottom, side and end forms configured to the approved T-girder geometry
- End forms or inserts reviewed for an approved family of related lengths
- Interfaces for reinforcement, prestressing, embedded items and lifting provisions
- Conventional, mechanical, hydraulic or movable operation selected for the production task
- Access for preparation, concrete placement, vibration, inspection, cleaning and maintenance

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Approved girder drawings, beam schedule, length range, target casting cycle, dimensional tolerance basis, prestressing information, concrete placement and curing methods, yard layout and handling plan.
