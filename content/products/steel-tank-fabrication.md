<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm contents, service conditions and design responsibility.**
2. **Prepare panels, openings and connection details.**
3. **Fit and weld the approved container geometry.**
4. **Complete specified cleaning and finishing.**
5. **Perform the agreed dimensional and integrity checks.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Pressure-rated or hazardous-fluid service requires an explicitly agreed design and acceptance basis.
