## Production scenarios and design basis

Stair and balcony moulds support building projects that need repeated components with controlled geometry, visible faces and connection details. The mould orientation and division are selected from riser and tread geometry, landing arrangement, balcony edge profile, drainage falls, inserts, finish requirements and handling method.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Contact surfaces formed to approved stair, landing or balcony geometry
- Adjustable end or side elements for an agreed family of variants
- Provision for connection hardware, lifting anchors and project-defined recesses
- Mould division planned for reinforcement access, casting and demoulding
- Surface preparation and inspection access for visible concrete faces

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Component drawings, variant schedule, visible-surface requirements, connection and lifting details, casting orientation, dimensional tolerances, production quantity and handling plan.
