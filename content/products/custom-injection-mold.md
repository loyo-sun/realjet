<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is injection moulding?</h2>

Injection moulding forms a specified resin in project tooling to create a component. The moulded-part design, resin behaviour and tooling scope must be defined together; it is a different production route from sheet-metal cutting and forming.

For this product, the process is applied to **produce nonmetallic equipment parts from an approved moulded-part design**. The particular review priorities are resin selection, parting lines, draft, shrinkage and tooling ownership; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Moulding and tooling workflow</h2>

1. **Review component geometry and resin requirements.**
2. **Define mould scope, ownership and release conditions.**
3. **Review draft, parting lines and critical features.**
4. **Produce trial parts and agree corrections.**
5. **Inspect approved samples before repeat production.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify the intended resin, appearance zones and required part tests; the tooling and component supply scopes are confirmed separately.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for custom injection moulded components can include customer-specified moulding resin after feasibility review. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Specify the resin, colour, texture and cosmetic faces. Material substitution and changes to mould geometry require approval; moulded-part tolerances depend on the resin, geometry and process.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom injection moulded components</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify resin selection, parting lines, draft, shrinkage and tooling ownership, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Identify the intended resin, appearance zones and required part tests; the tooling and component supply scopes are confirmed separately.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with customer-specified moulding resin after feasibility review. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

This supply concerns moulded parts and project tooling, separate from sheet-metal fabrication; confirm tooling and sample acceptance before production. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
