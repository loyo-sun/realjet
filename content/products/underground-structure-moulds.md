## Production scenarios and design basis

Underground structure moulds address components whose geometry does not fit a standard tunnel segment or culvert family. Typical applications may include shafts, utility galleries, access structures and project-defined underground modules, subject to confirmation from the approved design documents.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Modular steel panels configured around the approved structural envelope
- Internal cores, recess boxes and end forms planned for the required demoulding sequence
- Interfaces for penetrations, cast-in parts, joints and lifting provisions
- Mechanical or hydraulic assistance where justified by component size and operation
- Assembly references and inspection points for controlled repeat production

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Approved structural drawings, component schedule, penetrations and embedded-item details, joint design, casting and demoulding sequence, tolerance basis, lifting plan and site or factory constraints.
