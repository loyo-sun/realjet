## Concrete supply matched to mould feeding

It prepares the concrete supplied to the mould feeding station. Plant output, mix consistency and discharge timing are matched to the planned mould cycle.

Batching is planned around the concrete demand of each mould and the time available at the feeding station. The aggregate, binder, water and admixture systems need to suit the approved mix and the material supply available at the plant.

## Configuration and integration

- Aggregate storage and weighing arrangement
- Mixer batch volume and discharge interface
- Water and admixture dosing requirements
- Coordination with concrete transport and mould feeding

## Information for equipment selection

Concrete mix design, batch demand, pile output per shift, available footprint and material delivery method.

Realjet can include the concrete batching plant in a coordinated spun pile equipment package or review it for an existing production line. Equipment dimensions, capacity, controls and supply boundaries are confirmed against the project requirements before an equipment proposal is finalized.
