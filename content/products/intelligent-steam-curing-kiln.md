## Managed curing conditions

The kiln provides an enclosed environment for a defined curing programme. Chamber dimensions, door arrangement, steam distribution and monitoring points are configured around the component and production flow.

## Process integration

Curing is coordinated with mould circulation, transfer equipment, boiler capacity and the required release schedule. The control concept can record key process conditions according to the agreed project scope.

## Define the curing basis

Share component size, concrete mix, required strength development, target cycle, ambient conditions and available steam supply. The curing curve and acceptance method must be confirmed by the responsible project parties.
