## Formwork engineered for the component

The steel structure, working surfaces, hydraulic movement and adjustment points are developed from the approved component geometry and casting process. The design focuses on repeatable setup and practical access for production work.

## Operation and demoulding

Hydraulic functions can support opening, closing, alignment or demoulding according to the selected mould concept. Safety measures, maintenance access and control interfaces are defined for the actual production environment.

## Technical inputs

An initial review requires component drawings, concrete pressure assumptions, surface requirements, production cycle, lifting conditions and applicable standards. Final tolerances and acceptance criteria must be agreed in the technical specification.
