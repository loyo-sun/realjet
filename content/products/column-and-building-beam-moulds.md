## Production scenarios and design basis

These moulds are used for structural frames in industrial, commercial and multi-storey precast construction. The design basis includes column or beam cross-section, corbels and ledges, connection plates, reinforcement congestion, length variants, casting orientation and demoulding direction.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Side, bottom and end forms shaped to approved structural profiles
- Adjustable stops or end forms for an agreed range of component lengths
- Local inserts for corbels, ledges and approved connection details
- Locking and support arrangements based on the selected casting orientation
- Access for reinforcement placement, concrete consolidation and safe demoulding planning

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Structural component drawings, length schedule, corbel and connection details, reinforcement arrangement, tolerance basis, casting orientation, target cycle, lifting points and factory constraints.
