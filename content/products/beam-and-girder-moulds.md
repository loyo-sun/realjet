## Mould systems configured around the beam schedule

Beam and girder moulds are used in precast factories and project casting yards that manufacture repeated structural elements for highways, urban viaducts, rail infrastructure and industrial construction. The mould concept is developed from the approved component drawings, cross-section, length range, reinforcement and embedded-item arrangement, prestressing method where applicable, casting sequence and planned handling method.

Realjet can configure the mould around T-, I-, U-, trough or other project-defined beam and girder profiles. A typical system may combine a bottom form, side forms, end forms, supporting frames, locking points and working access. Mechanical or hydraulic movement is selected only where it supports the agreed opening, closing, positioning or demoulding sequence.

## Product performance and engineering features

- fabricated steel contact surfaces shaped to the approved component geometry;
- stiffened support frames designed around the mould layout and concrete pressure basis;
- reference points, stops and locking positions for controlled repeat setup;
- replaceable end forms or inserts where an approved family of related components must be produced;
- interfaces for reinforcement work, embedded items, concrete placement, vibration, curing and lifting operations;
- manual, mechanical or hydraulic side-form operation selected for the final production sequence;
- access for cleaning, release-agent application, inspection and routine maintenance;
- factory assembly and dimensional checks defined in the agreed inspection plan.

The mould does not determine the final component specification by itself. Concrete design, reinforcement, prestressing, tolerances, lifting points and acceptance criteria remain subject to the project engineer's approved documents and applicable standards.

## Suitable production scenarios

These moulds are relevant to permanent precast plants, temporary bridge casting yards and station-based production lines. They can support repeated production of bridge beams and girders when the mould, prestressing area, crane coverage, concrete supply, curing space and finished-product handling route are planned as one coordinated process.

For a technical proposal, provide the component drawings, production quantities, required cycle, dimensional tolerances, concrete placement method, prestressing information, curing process, available layout and local project requirements.
