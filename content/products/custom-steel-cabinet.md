<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm the equipment envelope and access arrangement.**
2. **Cut and form body panels and doors.**
3. **Join the shell and fit mounting details.**
4. **Apply the finish and install agreed hardware.**
5. **Verify panel fit, door operation and final dimensions.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Include the cabinet layout and the equipment installation arrangement, identifying which accessories are in scope.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom steel equipment cabinets</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify door construction, mounting plates and base or wall connections, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Include the cabinet layout and the equipment installation arrangement, identifying which accessories are in scope.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified steel sheet and cabinet hardware. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Review cabinet weight with equipment installed and specify the coating environment. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
