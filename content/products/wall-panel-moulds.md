## Production scenarios and design basis

Wall panel moulds serve residential, commercial and industrial building programmes that require repeated structural or architectural wall elements. Horizontal tables, battery-type arrangements or other project-specific concepts are selected from the panel geometry, surface requirements, insulation build-up where applicable, opening layout, embedded items and factory material flow.

## Is this mould relevant to your project?

This mould type may be relevant when:

- Structural, façade or sandwich panels form an approved repeat family
- Surface finish, openings, insulation or embedded items affect casting orientation
- Adjustable edges or inserts are needed for controlled panel variants
- Lifting, curing, stacking and material flow must fit the factory layout

## Configuration directions

| Configuration direction | When it may be considered | Main review focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal casting table | The required concrete face and factory handling suit flat production | Table size, edge forms, openings and lifting access |
| Battery-type or vertical arrangement | Approved panel geometry and production method support vertical casting | Cell width, access, closing, concrete placement and stripping |
| Adjustable or movable arrangement | Panel variants or factory flow require controlled repositioning | Adjustment range, reference points, utilities and transfer route |

## Product performance and engineering features

- Flat or shaped contact surfaces developed from the approved panel drawings
- Adjustable side rails, stops or inserts for an agreed panel family
- Interfaces for openings, embedded items and insulation layers where specified
- Support for the selected reinforcement, casting, vibration and curing sequence
- Accessible surfaces for preparation, cleaning and routine inspection

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Panel drawings and schedule, production orientation, thickness and opening ranges, surface finish, embedded-item and insulation details, target cycle, lifting method and factory layout.
