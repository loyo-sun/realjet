## Production scenarios and design basis

Box girder moulds support highway, railway, urban viaduct and long-span bridge projects using precast box-shaped superstructure elements. Web inclination, deck width, cell geometry, diaphragms, prestressing ducts, lifting provisions and casting orientation are reviewed before the mould is divided into practical bottom, side, end and internal-form assemblies.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Steel contact surfaces shaped to the approved external and internal girder geometry
- Mould segmentation planned around reinforcement installation, concrete access and demoulding clearance
- Reference points, stops and locking devices for controlled repeat setup
- Mechanical or hydraulic movement for selected side-form or internal-form functions where required
- Workshop trial assembly and dimensional verification to an agreed inspection plan

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Approved girder drawings, component schedule, casting orientation, prestressing and embedded-item details, target cycle, tolerance requirements, operating preference, yard layout and lifting method.
