<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Welding joins prepared metal parts into a component or subassembly. The joint design, access, material and sequence determine the practical process, while inspection should address the functional requirements of the finished assembly.

For this product, the process is applied to **join small metal parts into a repeatable fitted assembly**. The particular review priorities are fixture contact points, joint gaps and weld sequence; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm material, joints and acceptance requirements.**
2. **Prepare edges and fit the components.**
3. **Establish fixtures and the welding sequence.**
4. **Join parts and complete agreed post-weld operations.**
5. **Inspect welds and final assembly geometry.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify any required welding qualification, examination or documentation during the enquiry review.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for custom welded metal parts can include specified weldable metals. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Review coating, plating or a bare-metal finish against the specified material and environment. Identify appearance zones, threads, grounding contacts and fitted surfaces before defining masking and finishing requirements.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom welded metal parts</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify fixture contact points, joint gaps and weld sequence, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Identify any required welding qualification, examination or documentation during the enquiry review.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified weldable metals. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Agree which dimensions are checked on the free assembly and which use a checking fixture. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
