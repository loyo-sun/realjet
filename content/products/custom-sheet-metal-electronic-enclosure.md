<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review equipment layout and protection requirements.**
2. **Cut profiles and form the enclosure panels.**
3. **Join the body and prepare hardware positions.**
4. **Finish surfaces and fit specified covers or seals.**
5. **Inspect dimensions and perform agreed assembly tests.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. State protection targets and required test evidence before order acceptance; an example photo does not establish a rating.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for custom sheet metal electronic enclosures can include specified sheet metal and enclosure hardware. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Review coating, plating or a bare-metal finish against the specified material and environment. Identify appearance zones, threads, grounding contacts and fitted surfaces before defining masking and finishing requirements.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom sheet metal electronic enclosures</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify board clearance, ventilation and panel interface positions, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. State protection targets and required test evidence before order acceptance; an example photo does not establish a rating.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified sheet metal and enclosure hardware. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Confirm thermal and electrical design requirements with the equipment designer; the scope is the fabricated housing. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
