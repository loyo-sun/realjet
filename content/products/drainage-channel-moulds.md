<section class="wall-lp-section drainage-choice" id="mould-configurations">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="wall-lp-heading">
      <span class="eyebrow">We help choose the setup</span>
      <h2>The mould should fit your production, not just the drawing</h2>
      <p>Channel size, moving weight, casting frequency and available handling equipment determine how the mould should open.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="drainage-choice-layout">
      <figure>
        <div class="wall-lp-media-16x9"><img src="/images/products/precast-moulds/drainage-channel-moulds/05-open-side-form-mould-reference.webp" alt="Steel U-drain mould with its outer side panels opened for demoulding" width="1000" height="562" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></div>
        <figcaption><span>The opening path must release the concrete without trapping or damaging its edges.</span></figcaption>
      </figure>
      <div class="drainage-choice-list">
        <article><span>Manual</span><div><h3>Simple operator-controlled opening</h3><p>Considered when panels can be handled safely at the required production rate.</p></div></article>
        <article><span>Assisted</span><div><h3>Mechanical help for repeated movement</h3><p>Screw, linkage, rolling or lifting mechanisms can reduce handling effort.</p></div></article>
        <article><span>Hydraulic</span><div><h3>Controlled movement for heavier parts</h3><p>Considered when weight, frequency or release requirements justify powered operation.</p></div></article>
        <p class="drainage-choice-note"><strong>You do not need to choose now.</strong> We recommend a direction after reviewing your channel and factory process.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

</section>
