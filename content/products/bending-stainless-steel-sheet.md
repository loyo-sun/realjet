<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Bending changes a flat blank into an angled or multi-flange component. The final shape depends on material behaviour, bend allowance, tooling access and the sequence used to create connected bends.

For this product, the process is applied to **form stainless panels and brackets with defined surface appearance**. The particular review priorities are bend allowance, surface protection and mounting geometry; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm material, radius and formed dimensions.**
2. **Review the flat pattern and tooling access.**
3. **Prepare blanks and protect visible surfaces.**
4. **Form bends in the agreed sequence.**
5. **Inspect angles, offsets and final geometry.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Provide formed-part dimensions and datums as well as the flat pattern when both are available.

Start with specified stainless steel sheet. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.
