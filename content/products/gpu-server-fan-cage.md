## Fan Cages for GPU Servers to your design

Order the metal frame that locates fan modules in your air-cooled or hybrid-cooled GPU server. REALJET makes the cage to your aperture layout, connection details and chassis mounting drawing.

Supply is the metal cage and listed supports or fasteners. Fan motors, wiring, control electronics and plastic ducts are included only under a separate agreed scope.

<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

Small slot errors can accumulate across a multi-fan cage. Define each aperture, slot pitch, seating stop and chassis fixing point. Show the module removal path and the space needed to operate its latch.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Establish the complete slot layout

Review aperture webs, bend access and the common pitch datum before blank preparation. Keep all fan slots tied to the same drawing reference.

### Form and connect the cage

Plan forming before obstructing returns are joined. Review how frame connections and local restraint may move the narrow aperture walls.

### Check after finishing

Deburr module entry edges and review coating on stops and sliding-contact areas. Inspect the finished cage, not only the uncoated blank.

<h2 id="inspection">Define what the first article must demonstrate</h2>

| Feature | Proposed check | Record to agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slot pitch and aperture | Measure each aperture and its location from the common datum; do not add separate slot-width checks as a substitute for cumulative pitch. | Per-slot dimensions and datum-based positions. |
| Module seating | Use the specified fan module or gauge in every slot; check stop engagement and fixing alignment. | Slot-by-slot fit checklist identifying the module/gauge. |
| Maintenance removal | Check module removal and latch access with the neighbouring slots occupied as defined in the test arrangement. | Removal observations, edge condition and test arrangement. |

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Support the frame so narrow aperture walls and seating tabs do not take transport loads. Identify the fan-module variant on the part and packing list as agreed. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Why check every fan slot?

A module can fit one slot while another is affected by pitch, forming or joining variation. The first-article plan should record each aperture and position, then check seating in each slot with the nominated module or gauge.

### Do you supply the fans with the cage?

The standard enquiry scope is the metal frame. List any purchased fan modules or hardware separately if you want them included in the quotation.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
