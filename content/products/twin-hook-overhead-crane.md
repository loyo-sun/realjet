## Two-point handling of long pile moulds

A twin-hook overhead crane handles long spun pile moulds at two defined lifting points. Hook arrangement, lifting accessories and travel coverage must work with the mould lifting plan and the production bay.

Long moulds need a lifting arrangement that supports the load at the specified points. The two hooks, lifting accessories and operating controls should be coordinated with the mould lifting plan and the clearances between production stations.

## Configuration and integration

- Total load and load distribution between hooks
- Bay span, lifting height and travel coverage
- Hook spacing and coordinated movement
- Runway, lifting accessories and control interfaces

## Information for equipment selection

Loaded mould mass, lifting-point drawings, building dimensions, runway information and production lifting sequence.

## Selection questions

### Can total mould weight simply be divided equally between two hooks?

Equal loading should not be assumed. The centre of gravity and lifting-point arrangement determine the load at each hook, and the crane selection must follow the agreed lifting plan.

### What building information is needed before crane selection?

Provide bay span, available headroom, required lift height, runway information and the stations to be served. Include the mould lifting points and accessories so that clearances can be checked across the complete movement.
