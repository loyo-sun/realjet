## Pulling equipment for a defined transfer route

A material transfer winch supplies pulling force along an agreed route in the spun pile plant. It may serve a trolley or another defined transfer duty, with the rope path and anchoring coordinated to the layout.

A winch can provide the pulling force for a trolley or another agreed material-transfer application. Rope routing, travel distance and load resistance determine the arrangement; the supplied configuration must be matched to the intended pulling duty.

## Configuration and integration

- Required line pull and operating duty
- Rope capacity and travel distance
- Drum, guide and anchoring arrangement
- Braking, travel limits and control positions

## Information for equipment selection

Moving load, rolling resistance or gradient, route length, rope routing and required operating speed.

## Selection questions

### Is the winch specified here for overhead lifting?

This page describes a pulling application. Any lifting application requires its own equipment selection and confirmed load, braking and operating requirements; a pulling arrangement should not be assumed suitable for lifting.

### Can line pull be selected from trolley mass alone?

No. Route gradients, rolling resistance, starting conditions and rope routing affect the pulling requirement. Provide the moving load and route information together with the required speed and operating frequency.
