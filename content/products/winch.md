## Pulling equipment for a defined transfer route

It pulls trolleys or materials along a defined transfer path where required. Pulling load, travel distance and safety devices are set to the application.

A winch can provide the pulling force for a trolley or another agreed material-transfer application. Rope routing, travel distance and load resistance determine the arrangement; the supplied configuration must be matched to the intended pulling duty.

## Configuration and integration

- Required line pull and operating duty
- Rope capacity and travel distance
- Drum, guide and anchoring arrangement
- Braking, travel limits and control positions

## Information for equipment selection

Moving load, rolling resistance or gradient, route length, rope routing and required operating speed.

## Supply and project coordination

Realjet can include the winch in a coordinated spun pile equipment package or review it for an existing production line. Equipment dimensions, capacity, controls and supply boundaries are confirmed against the project requirements before an equipment proposal is finalized.
