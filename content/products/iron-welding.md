<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm material, joints and acceptance requirements.**

4. **Join parts and complete agreed post-weld operations.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify any required welding qualification, examination or documentation during the enquiry review.
