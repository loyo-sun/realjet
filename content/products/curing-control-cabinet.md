## Temperature monitoring for the curing process

A curing control cabinet brings temperature measurements and curing-stage operation into a central panel. For a spun pile plant, its configuration depends on the curing pits or chambers and the agreed steam-control scope.

The cabinet brings curing temperature measurements and operating stages into one control point. The number of curing zones, sensor positions and steam-valve interfaces determine the control scope, including whether recording or automatic stage control is required.

## Configuration and integration

- Number of curing pits or chambers
- Temperature sensor positions and channels
- Steam-valve and alarm interfaces
- Recipe, recording and operator-display requirements

## Information for equipment selection

Curing layout, temperature schedule, zone count, steam-control arrangement and required production records.

## Selection questions

### Does the cabinet include the complete steam-curing system?

The cabinet is the monitoring and control component. Sensors, steam valves, piping, curing enclosures and the boiler must be listed in the project scope, including which components are supplied and connected by each party.

### Does reaching the temperature setpoint authorize demoulding?

Temperature records support process control but do not by themselves establish concrete release strength. Stress release and demoulding follow the approved curing procedure and the project quality requirements.
