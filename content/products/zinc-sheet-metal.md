<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm stock grade and component function.**
2. **Cut profiles and prepare connection features.**
3. **Form or join the specified sections.**
4. **Complete machining or finishing where required.**
5. **Inspect final geometry and protective treatment.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify the material grade precisely and describe any load-bearing or corrosion-related requirements.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about galvanized sheet metal fabrication</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify cut-edge condition, formed corners and joining method, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Identify the material grade precisely and describe any load-bearing or corrosion-related requirements.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified galvanized steel sheet. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Review coating damage at bends and joints and agree any local coating repair. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
