## Production scenarios and design basis

Segmental bridge moulds are used in casting yards producing segments for span-by-span, balanced-cantilever and other approved erection methods. The mould concept follows the bridge alignment, segment family, casting curve, match-casting procedure, diaphragm and web arrangement, shear-key details, post-tensioning ducts and survey-control method.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Bottom, side and bulkhead structures configured to the approved segment geometry
- Fixed and adjustable references for the selected short-line or long-line method
- Provisions for match-cast positioning and survey-control points
- Interfaces for shear keys, ducts, embedded items and lifting provisions shown on approved drawings
- Mechanical or hydraulic positioning functions selected for the agreed casting sequence

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Segment drawings, bridge alignment data, casting and erection methods, segment schedule, tolerance basis, duct and embedded-item details, target cycle, yard arrangement and survey procedure.
