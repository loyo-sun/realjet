<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Forming changes local or overall sheet geometry to create tabs, flanges, channels or other features. Feature interaction and the way the part sits against its mating component determine the practical inspection plan.

For this product, the process is applied to **produce a single equipment part with application-specific cut and formed features**. The particular review priorities are functional edges, tabs and assembly clearances; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review the feature geometry and material formability.**
2. **Define blanks and any local tooling.**
3. **Cut and prepare profiles.**
4. **Form and deburr the specified features.**
5. **Check feature location and mating fit.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify whether dimensions apply to the free part or to a part held in its assembly position.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for custom sheet metal parts can include specified formable metal stock. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Review coating, plating or a bare-metal finish against the specified material and environment. Identify appearance zones, threads, grounding contacts and fitted surfaces before defining masking and finishing requirements.

<h2 id="quality">Inspection and quality documentation</h2>

Identify the surfaces that locate the part in its final assembly and inspect from those datums. The inspection plan should address **formed-feature locations, edge condition and attachment surfaces**, with critical dimensions and test methods identified before production release.

Agree whether the order requires material identification records, dimensional reports, sample approval or additional tests. Any specified certification, qualification or compliance evidence is reviewed for the actual supply scope before order acceptance. Packaging should protect finished surfaces and the geometric features used to install the part.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom sheet metal parts</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify functional edges, tabs and assembly clearances, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Identify whether dimensions apply to the free part or to a part held in its assembly position.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified formable metal stock. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Identify the surfaces that locate the part in its final assembly and inspect from those datums. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
