<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Establish module and enclosure datums

Review support attachments, base members and door opening together. Identify purchased sections and weld access before frame preparation.

### Control geometry through joining

Review tack-up and weld sequence with restraint on functional interfaces. Plan free-state checks after release, rather than fitting the door to a frame held square in a fixture.

### Prepare panel and support interfaces

Review any post-weld machining and finishing. Coating allowance, masked contacts and the sequence of panel fitting follow the cabinet design.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Identify the permitted lifting and restraint locations. Protect the door opening and support datums, and confirm whether attachments should be installed before shipment. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### When should the door opening be checked?

Check it after frame joining and fixture release, and review it again where finish or installed supports affect fit. A door fitted only while the frame is restrained does not establish the delivered frame condition.

### Can you make a frame for our battery module arrangement?

Yes. Send the cabinet assembly, module-support interfaces and loading requirements. The frame quotation follows that approved design rather than a universal module layout.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
