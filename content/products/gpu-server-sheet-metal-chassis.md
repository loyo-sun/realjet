## GPU Server Sheet Metal Chassis to your design

Source the bottom shell, structural panels or a joined metal chassis structure for your AI training or inference server. REALJET reviews your part drawings with the GPU tray, fan, power supply and rack interfaces before the manufacturing scope is agreed.

<h2 id="supply">Choose the part and delivery condition</h2>

| Your order | Information that sets the scope |
| --- | --- |
| Chassis bottom shell | Flat pattern or formed drawing, datums and mounting features |
| Structural panel set | Individual part drawings, quantities and connections |

The order defines which panels arrive joined, which arrive loose and which hardware is included. GPU boards, fans, power supplies and server validation are separate from the metal chassis order unless explicitly agreed.

Show the common shell datums, tray support heights, fan opening positions and rail attachments on the assembly drawing. Identify removal directions so a dimensionally correct shell does not obstruct the hardware during service.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Lay out the shell

Review bend access and hole positions against the flat pattern before blank preparation. Resolve openings or fasteners close to a bend before forming.

### Form and locate the panels

Plan the bend order around tool access. Use the shell datum to locate structural panels; compare the open shell geometry before and after joining.

### Finish the assembly interfaces

Review whether tray supports need machining after joining. Protect rack attachments and module entry edges during the specified finish.

<h2 id="inspection">Define what the first article must demonstrate</h2>

| Feature | Proposed check | Record to agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shell envelope and twist | Support the shell at the drawing datums; measure opposing corners, heights and panel offsets. A diagonal check alone does not establish hole position. | Envelope readings, support arrangement and drawing revision. |
| Tray, fan and rack interfaces | Measure fixing positions from the common datums, then trial-fit nominated mating parts or gauges in the finished shell. | Recorded hole/support dimensions plus separate fit observations. |
| Access and finished edges | Check cover fastening, tray removal and fan insertion paths using the agreed components. Inspect exposed edges and masked contacts. | Assembly checklist and any agreed finish observations. |

Identify the drawing revision, measurement datums and pass/fail limits before the sample is made. The quote confirms the available measuring setup, any mating fixture and whether a specialist test needs an external provider.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Protect module entry edges and mounting surfaces. Specify loose panel sets or joined chassis so the packing supports and shipping volume can be reviewed. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### How do you keep the panels aligned after joining?

The drawing review establishes a common shell datum, the panel locating arrangement and a joining sequence. The first article should be measured after joining and again where finishing may affect the mating interfaces. Tooling and measurement equipment are confirmed in the quote.

### Can I order the shell without the complete chassis?

Yes. Identify the shell drawing and mating interfaces. Panels, reinforcement and purchased hardware are included only when they are listed in the quotation.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
