## Solar Inverter Wall Mounting Brackets to your design

Order the metal wall bracket that supports your solar PV inverter at its rear mounting interface. REALJET makes the contour, formed hooks and fixing slots to your inverter and installation drawings.

The order covers the bracket and explicitly listed hardware. The inverter, wall anchors and installation work are separate unless agreed. Support loads and anchor requirements follow your approved installation design.

<h2 id="interfaces">Fit it to the equipment around it</h2>

Wall-side fixings and the inverter hanging interface must be reviewed together. Show hook engagement, locking provisions and the clearance needed to lift the inverter into position. Identify the substrate and anchors in your installation design; they are not selected from the bracket photograph.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Relate wall and device interfaces

Review slot positions, hook engagement and the installed offset together. Resolve bend-adjacent openings before preparing the blank.

### Form hooks and join specified features

Plan bend access around the hanging features. If features are welded, review their locating arrangement and check the hook relationship after joining.

### Finish for the specified environment

Confirm material and corrosion-protection route in the quote. Recheck hook engagement after finishing where coating allowance affects fit.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Should hook fit be checked before or after coating?

Both stages may be useful, but acceptance must address the delivered condition. Where coating build-up affects engagement or locking, use the nominated mating interface for the finished-bracket check.

### Can you make a bracket for a particular inverter model?

Provide the approved mounting-interface drawing or a dimensioned design. Include the inverter envelope and hanging details; appearance alone does not establish compatibility.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
