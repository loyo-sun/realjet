## Production scenarios and design basis

These moulds are used by drainage and utility-component producers manufacturing circular, rectangular or project-specific access chambers. The configuration follows internal and external geometry, base channels, wall penetrations, joint profiles, riser sizes, lifting details and the intended casting orientation.

## Product performance and engineering features

- External shells and internal cores configured to approved chamber geometry
- Opening boxes and inserts for documented pipe entries and connection details
- Core withdrawal concept selected from geometry, clearance and operating sequence
- End and joint forms for compatible bases, risers or cover units where required
- Cleaning, inspection and maintenance access incorporated into the operating layout

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Chamber drawings, base and riser schedule, penetration details, joint profiles, casting orientation, tolerance basis, target cycle, lifting method and factory handling constraints.
