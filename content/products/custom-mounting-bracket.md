<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

A fabricated bracket uses cut profiles, bends and sometimes welded reinforcement to connect two mounting interfaces. Its function depends on the relationship between those interfaces and the loads transmitted through the installation.

For this product, the process is applied to **locate an equipment component between two defined mounting interfaces**. The particular review priorities are fastener pattern, offset and installation clearance; those details connect the manufacturing steps to the equipment's assembly requirements.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review mating parts, load direction and attachment method.**
2. **Profile the blank and prepare hole features.**
3. **Form the connection faces and add specified reinforcement.**
4. **Complete joining and surface treatment.**
5. **Check angles, offsets and hole positions against datums.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Provide loading and mounting information where structural performance is part of the component design.
