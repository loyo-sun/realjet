<h2 id="sheet-metal-fabrication">What is sheet metal fabrication for this application?</h2>

Stainless fabrication combines cutting, forming or welding with control of the final surface condition. Grade selection and finishing depend on the actual exposure and appearance requirements, including the condition of joints and cut edges.

<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Confirm stainless grade and finish requirements.**
2. **Cut profiles and prepare edges.**
3. **Form or join parts to the approved drawing.**
4. **Complete agreed post-fabrication treatment.**
5. **Inspect geometry and final surface condition.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Describe exposure and cleaning requirements; stainless steel is a material family rather than a single corrosion-performance specification.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for custom stainless steel formed parts can include specified stainless sheet or plate. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Specify grain direction, appearance zones and any post-weld treatment. Brushing, polishing or passivation can be reviewed when appropriate to the selected grade and service.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about custom stainless steel formed parts</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify flange geometry, visible faces and fastener placement, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Describe exposure and cleaning requirements; stainless steel is a material family rather than a single corrosion-performance specification.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified stainless sheet or plate. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Agree finish direction and verify the locations of holes on different formed faces. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
