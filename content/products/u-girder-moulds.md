## Production scenarios and design basis

U-girder moulds are used where the component geometry requires coordinated inner, outer, bottom and end-form movement. The concept is reviewed against the approved section, variable geometry, reinforcement and embedded items, concrete placement, demoulding clearance and component-handling plan.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Inner and outer forms configured to the approved U-section
- Opening and withdrawal sequence reviewed against available demoulding space
- End forms and inserts developed for the agreed girder family
- Mechanical, hydraulic, rail-guided or station-based movement reviewed as project options
- Access provisions for preparation, placement, vibration, inspection and cleaning

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Approved U-girder drawings, section and length schedule, reinforcement and embedded-item details, prestressing information where applicable, target cycle, movement envelope, factory layout and lifting or transfer plan.
