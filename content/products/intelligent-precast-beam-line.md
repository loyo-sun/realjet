## Integrated around the beam production cycle

The line is configured around the precast component, mould strategy, daily output target, curing method and material flow. Individual machines are coordinated as a production system rather than selected as isolated equipment.

## Project-specific configuration

The final arrangement can combine forming, reinforcement preparation, concrete distribution, vibration, curing, demoulding and transfer functions. Equipment scope, automation level and interfaces are defined from approved project requirements.

## Information needed for an initial proposal

Share component drawings, planned quantities, target cycle, available site area, utilities and applicable project standards. Realjet can then develop an initial process concept and equipment boundary for technical discussion.
