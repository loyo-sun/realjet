## Reinforcement cages for spun concrete piles

A spun pile cage welding machine positions longitudinal reinforcement and welds the spiral wire to form a cylindrical cage. It connects PC bar preparation with end-plate assembly and mould loading.

Longitudinal bar positioning and spiral-wire placement determine how the cage fits inside the mould. Cage production should be coordinated with bar preparation, end assembly and the handling route to the mould-loading station.

## Configuration and integration

- Cage diameter and usable production length
- Longitudinal bar count and positioning
- Spiral pitch and wire feed arrangement
- Welding setup and cage discharge supports

## Information for equipment selection

Cage drawings, bar and wire specifications, spiral pitch schedule, cage lengths and required output.

## Selection questions

### What is needed to select a cage welding machine?

Provide the cage diameter and length, longitudinal bar count and sizes, spiral-wire specification and pitch schedule. State which cage families will share the machine so that tooling changes and the discharge arrangement can be reviewed.

### Does cage welding apply prestress to the pile?

No. Cage welding assembles the reinforcement. Prestress is applied at the tensioning stage through the approved anchorage and mould-end arrangement. The cage geometry must fit those downstream interfaces.
