## A shared interface across forming, curing and handling

It carries the cage and concrete through tensioning, spinning and curing. Diameter, length, running rings and lifting points are coordinated with the line equipment.

A split steel mould determines the external pile geometry while carrying the reinforcement and fresh concrete. Its running rings, end assemblies and closure details must work with the selected spinning machine, tensioning equipment and lifting arrangement.

## Configuration and integration

- Pile diameter, length and end geometry
- Shell joints, alignment and closure fasteners
- Running-ring diameter and roller positions
- Lifting points and curing-route compatibility

## Information for equipment selection

Pile drawings, product size schedule, loaded mass, spinning-machine interfaces and planned curing and handling methods.

## Supply and project coordination

Realjet can include the spun pile steel mould in a coordinated spun pile equipment package or review it for an existing production line. Equipment dimensions, capacity, controls and supply boundaries are confirmed against the project requirements before an equipment proposal is finalized.
