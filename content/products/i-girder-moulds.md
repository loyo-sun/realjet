## Production scenarios and design basis

I-girder moulds support repeat production in precast factories or dedicated casting yards. Engineering begins with the approved flange and web geometry, girder height and length range, end details, reinforcement, prestressing system, lifting arrangement and casting sequence.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Side, bottom and end forms matched to the approved I-girder section
- Stiffened frames and locking positions developed from the mould design basis
- Replaceable end details or inserts where an approved component family permits adjustment
- Mechanical or hydraulic assistance reviewed against moving mass and target cycle
- Working access for reinforcement, concrete placement, inspection and maintenance

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Approved I-girder drawings, component schedule, prestressing and embedded-item details, target output, casting and curing method, available operating space, lifting plan and factory or yard layout.
