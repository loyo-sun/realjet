<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

1. **Review frame layout, load basis and mounting references.**
2. **Prepare sections, plates or formed members.**
3. **Fit and join parts using the agreed sequence.**
4. **Machine or finish designated interfaces.**
5. **Check diagonals, mounting planes and assembly fit.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Provide the approved structural arrangement and load cases where required; rated performance must be established for that design.

<h2 id="materials-finishes">Materials, finish and delivered condition</h2>

Material selection for welded steel machine structures can include specified structural steel members and plate. The choice must reflect the actual application, rather than relying on the appearance or colour of the example image.

Review coating, plating or a bare-metal finish against the specified material and environment. Identify appearance zones, threads, grounding contacts and fitted surfaces before defining masking and finishing requirements.

<h2 id="quality">Inspection and quality documentation</h2>

Identify which surfaces need post-weld machining and which remain as fabricated. The inspection plan should address **frame alignment, joint acceptance and mounting datums**, with critical dimensions and test methods identified before production release.

Any specified certification, qualification or compliance evidence is reviewed for the actual supply scope before order acceptance. Packaging should protect finished surfaces and the geometric features used to install the part.

<h2 id="component-faq">Questions about welded steel machine structures</h2>

### What should I show on the drawing?

Identify connection locations, weld access and final geometric references, together with the part number, revision and mating references. Provide a dimensioned PDF and available STEP, DXF or DWG files as appropriate to the component. Provide the approved structural arrangement and load cases where required; rated performance must be established for that design.

### Which material and finish should I request?

Start with specified structural steel members and plate. Describe the operating environment and identify any functional or visible surfaces. The material, manufacturing route and finish are reviewed together before the quotation is finalized.

### What needs particular attention on this project?

Identify which surfaces need post-weld machining and which remain as fabricated. State the acceptance method for any critical characteristic, and confirm whether the order includes only the component or additional hardware and assembly operations.

### Can I order an initial sample before repeat production?

A prototype or pilot batch can be reviewed against the design and requested quantity. Agree sample acceptance, any required test evidence and the revision to be used for the subsequent order. Lead time is confirmed after the scope and material availability are checked.
