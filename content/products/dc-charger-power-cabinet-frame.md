<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Locate module bays and base connections

Review the cabinet datum scheme, member preparation and panel attachment points against the power-module arrangement.

### Review frame joining

Plan tack-up, weld access and restraint with particular attention to tall module bays. Inspect geometry after release from the fixture.

### Prepare the enclosure interfaces

Review any mounting-interface machining and the finish for the installation environment. Frame fabrication and completed outdoor-enclosure qualification remain separate scopes.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### Should the frame be inspected with its panels pulling it into shape?

The delivered frame condition and panel fit should be distinguished. The review defines where free-state geometry is required, then checks that the specified panels and modules fit without concealing frame misalignment.

### Can the frame suit a modular DC charging layout?

Yes. Provide the power-module arrangement, outer-panel interfaces and service clearances. Each cabinet variant follows its approved drawing.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
