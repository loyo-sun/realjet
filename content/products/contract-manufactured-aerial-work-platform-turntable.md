## Made-to-drawing turntable structures for access equipment

An aerial work platform turntable is the structural connection between the chassis, slewing system and upper working equipment. Realjet provides machinery contract manufacturing for project-specific turntable structures, producing each assembly to the customer's approved drawings, material requirements and inspection plan.

The representative component shown here combines cut and formed plates, a load-bearing welded structure, connection brackets and controlled mounting interfaces. It is a manufacturing reference rather than an off-the-shelf model; dimensions, materials, loads and interface details are confirmed separately for every project.

## Manufacturing scope and controls

- CNC laser, plasma or flame cutting selected for the plate grade and thickness;
- bending and forming of structural plates and brackets;
- fixture-controlled welding to manage geometry and distortion;
- drilling, boring or other machining of critical holes and mounting interfaces;
- weld, dimensional and final-condition inspection against the approved quality plan;
- phosphating, painting or powder coating according to the specified finish.

For construction machinery component manufacturing, the key challenge is not simply completing individual processes. The welded datum structure, slewing interface, pin positions and equipment mounting points must remain coordinated after fabrication and machining. Realjet plans the process route around those relationships and records the required checks before delivery.

## Typical applications

This contract-manufactured structure can support boom lifts, scissor lifts, telehandlers and other mobile access or construction equipment when the buyer needs a qualified partner for fabricated and machined steel assemblies. Supply can cover a prototype, a repeat production batch or an assembly prepared for installation into the customer's machine.
