## Station-based production flow

Mobile casting beds move between planned work areas so labour, tools and process equipment can be organised by station. The circulation concept is selected according to the component family, cycle time and available factory layout.

## System coordination

Transfer equipment, rails, workstations, curing positions and control logic are developed together. Layout review includes movement envelopes, maintenance access, crane coverage and interfaces with concrete supply.

## Planning basis

Share component dimensions, weight, required output, shift pattern, site layout and preferred curing process. These inputs establish the number of beds, station sequence and initial circulation logic.
