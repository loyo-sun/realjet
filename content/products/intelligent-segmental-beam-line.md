## Production flow for segmental components

The production concept connects mould preparation, reinforcement, embedded items, concrete placement, curing and segment handling. The arrangement is developed around the selected casting method and the geometry-control requirements of the project.

## Controlled interfaces

Moulds, survey points, concrete supply, lifting routes and curing areas are planned as connected interfaces. This helps the project team review how each segment moves through production before civil works and equipment positions are fixed.

## Define the required scope

Provide segment drawings, match-casting requirements, production schedule, available cranes and site constraints. The final equipment and service scope is confirmed through the agreed technical specification.
