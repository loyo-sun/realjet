Source a metal bracket that fixes your heat exchanger to its cooling-equipment frame. REALJET makes the non-wetted support to your mounting pattern, stand-off dimensions and connection details.

<h2 id="manufacturing">Plan the manufacturing around these features</h2>

The steps below are proposed for the drawing review. Your material, geometry and acceptance requirements determine the final route, tooling and inspection provision.

### Review offsets against the exchanger

Review bend radii, fixing holes and tool access against the mounting-interface drawing. Identify handed parts and their installed spacing.

### Form or join the specified support

Use a forming route for a bent bracket or review restraint and joining order for a welded support. Do not assume the same route suits both designs.

### Finish the mounting contacts

Review any required face/slot finishing after joining. Surface treatment must preserve the specified fit and corrosion requirements.

## Packing for your next assembly step

Label left and right parts where needed and protect mounting faces. Pack slender offsets so they cannot be bent by the weight of other components. State the quantity and delivery city so packing volume and the requested delivery terms can be included.

<h2 id="faq">Questions before you send drawings</h2>

### How do you check a handed bracket pair?

Identify both parts and establish their common frame-side datums. The inspection plan can check offsets separately and the installed spacing as a pair, using the nominated exchanger interface or fixture.

### Is the bracket in contact with coolant?

The proposed product is a non-wetted mounting bracket. Any coolant-contact or pressure-containing part needs a separate material, joining and test review.

### What should I send for the drawing review?

Send the dimensioned PDF, available STEP/DXF/DWG files and assembly view. Include material, finish, quantity, delivery city and drawing revision. Mark the features in the inspection plan that control acceptance.

### Can we approve a sample before ordering repeat quantities?

Request a sample or pilot batch with the agreed dimensional report and any mating checks. Scope, timing and test provision are quoted after review; repeat supply follows the accepted drawing revision and any agreed sample changes.
