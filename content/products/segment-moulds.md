## Mould systems for match-cast bridge segments

Segment moulds are used in casting yards that manufacture precast concrete segments for span-by-span, balanced-cantilever and other segmental bridge erection methods. Depending on the approved construction method, segments may be produced on a short-line or long-line basis. Match casting uses the previously cast segment as one reference face for the next segment so that the joint geometry can be controlled for erection.

The mould concept is developed from the bridge geometry, segment family, casting curve, match-casting procedure, diaphragm and web arrangement, shear-key details, post-tensioning ducts, embedded items and survey-control method. The system is project-specific because alignment and geometry requirements change along the bridge and between segment types.

## Product performance and engineering features

- fabricated steel bottom, side and bulkhead structures configured to the approved segment geometry;
- fixed and adjustable references for the planned short-line or long-line casting method;
- provisions for match-cast positioning, alignment checks and survey-control points;
- interfaces for shear keys, post-tensioning ducts, embedded items and lifting provisions shown on approved drawings;
- mechanical or hydraulic opening and positioning functions selected for the agreed operating sequence;
- stiffened frames, locking devices and supports based on the mould design basis;
- working access for reinforcement, concrete placement, vibration, inspection, cleaning and maintenance;
- trial assembly and dimensional inspection requirements agreed before production release.

The mould supports the casting geometry but does not replace bridge geometry control. Segment positioning, survey calculations, match-cast records, concrete acceptance, prestressing and erection remain part of the project's engineering and quality system.

## Suitable production scenarios

Segment moulds are suitable for bridge casting yards serving elevated roads, metro viaducts, railway bridges, sea-crossing approaches and other projects using precast segmental construction. The mould layout should be coordinated with reinforcement preparation, concrete supply, curing, segment turning or lifting, survey activities, storage and delivery to the erection front.

For concept development, provide the segment drawings, bridge alignment information, casting method, segment schedule, required tolerances, duct and embedded-item details, target cycle, yard layout and applicable inspection requirements.
