<h2 id="fabrication-technology">Fabrication technology and production sequence</h2>

2. **Cut profiles and prepare connection features.**
3. **Form or join the specified sections.**
4. **Complete machining or finishing where required.**

The final route is selected during technical review. Additional operations, purchased fittings and testing are included only where agreed in the order scope. Identify the material grade precisely and describe any load-bearing or corrosion-related requirements.
