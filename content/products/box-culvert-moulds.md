## Production scenarios and design basis

Box culvert moulds are used for drainage crossings, utility corridors, underpasses and other buried infrastructure. The design is coordinated with internal clear dimensions, wall and slab thickness, joint profile, cell arrangement, reinforcement access, casting orientation and lifting method.

## Is this mould relevant to your project?

This mould type may be relevant when:

- Approved culvert geometry defines one or more cells, joint profiles or size variants
- The internal core requires a planned collapse, withdrawal or movement sequence
- Reinforcement access and concrete placement must be coordinated with the mould division
- Repeat production must fit the available lifting equipment and yard layout

## Configuration directions

| Configuration direction | When it may be considered | Main review focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed or segmented core and outer panels | Suitable access and demoulding clearance are available | Panel joints, lifting points, assembly sequence and sealing |
| Collapsible or mechanically assisted core | Internal geometry restricts direct core withdrawal | Collapse path, clearance, locking and operating access |
| Hydraulic or movable arrangement | Larger sections or repeated operation justify powered movement | Stroke, support, controls, safety and maintenance interfaces |

## Product performance and engineering features

- External panels and internal core sections configured to the approved culvert geometry
- Collapsible, movable or fixed core concepts selected from demoulding clearance and cycle requirements
- End-form provisions for approved joint profiles and dimensional variants
- Support and locking systems based on the mould layout and concrete pressure basis
- Access for reinforcement work, concrete placement, consolidation, cleaning and inspection

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Culvert drawings, cell configuration, joint details, size schedule, reinforcement arrangement, casting orientation, target cycle, lifting method, yard layout and tolerance requirements.
