## Production scenarios and design basis

Concrete pipe moulds are configured for drainage, sewer, water-management and utility applications. Diameter, wall thickness, pipe length, socket and spigot geometry, reinforcement arrangement, casting orientation, compaction method and lifting process determine the mould concept.

## Product performance and engineering features

- Inner and outer mould surfaces configured to approved pipe dimensions
- End rings or joint formers for specified socket, spigot or project-defined connections
- Core and shell opening arrangements selected for the agreed production process
- Alignment and locking features for controlled assembly
- Handling and maintenance provisions coordinated with the factory workflow

Final component tolerances and acceptance criteria remain subject to the approved project documents, production controls and applicable standards.

## Required project inputs

Pipe drawings, diameter and length schedule, wall thickness, joint profile, reinforcement method, casting and compaction process, tolerance basis, target cycle and handling equipment.
