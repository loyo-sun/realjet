<h2 id="applications">Where the data center power equipment mounting frame fits</h2>

### Mechanical bases for prefabricated electrical equipment

Source a custom power skid frame for data-center switchgear or UPS equipment, fabricated to your approved cabinet layout. REALJET supplies the steel mounting structure for your integration work. Cabinet spacing, fixing patterns and cable-entry positions define the frame; wiring and electrical tests remain with the equipment integrator.

### Cable access and off-site assembly

If the equipment is assembled before shipment, the layout must allow access to fasteners, cable openings and approved handling points. Where transport requires a split frame, the splice and locating details must preserve the intended cabinet relationships after reassembly.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Prepare cabinet supports and cable openings

Cut plates and members from the approved arrangement. Form specified brackets and remove burrs from cable-contact edges. Check that supports and bracing do not obstruct cable routes or installation tools.

### Weld and trial-assemble the structure

Locate the cabinet seating zones in a fixture and control weld sequence around the open bays. Trial-assemble any shipping sections before finish, record their orientation and mark the mating parts.

### Finish the mounting and bonding interfaces

Machine drawing-defined seating faces and holes where required. Mask designated bonding contacts and threads through the selected coating route, then recheck cabinet fixing features.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cabinet seating zones | Support heights, cabinet-to-cabinet spacing and fixing holes | Datum-based frame report |
| Cable routes and split joints | Opening clearances, edge condition, splice alignment and tool access | Template fit and trial-assembly record |
| Bonding and handling details | Specified contact locations, masked surfaces and approved lifting features | Mechanical checklist; continuity testing only with agreed method and provision |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

This order covers the mechanical frame. Cabinet installation, wiring and electrical tests require a separate integrator scope.

### Can you supply a frame for several cabinets?

Send each cabinet interface and the common assembly layout. The drawing review establishes spacing, support, cable access and whether trial assembly is required.
