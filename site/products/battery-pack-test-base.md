### Mechanical support beneath a pack test station

Order a custom mechanical base for battery pack end-of-line (EOL) test equipment. REALJET fabricates the weldment and machines the specified pack-support, locating-fixture and conveyor interfaces to your drawings. ### Fixture exchange and connector access

If the station handles different packs through exchangeable fixtures, identify the common locating datums and the mechanical envelope of each changeover. Leave access for connector maintenance and fixture removal before adding crossmembers or guard supports.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Locate pack and transfer interfaces together

Cut the members and plates from the pack-support and conveyor arrangement. Include drawing-defined connector-fixture and guard supports, checking that their fasteners remain accessible.

### Weld and finish the locating system

Review distortion and any stability treatment before machining fixture seats, dowel bores and conveyor interfaces. Removable supports use the approved mechanical locators rather than relying on clearance bolts for repeat positioning.

### Prepare for unpowered trial fitting

Deburr and clean the structure, mask specified contacts and finish to the agreed coating system. Use an unpowered dummy or checking fixture for transfer, locating and interference checks.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Can you check the base without a live battery pack?

Yes. Agree an unpowered dummy pack, mating template or fixture so transfer, seating and access can be checked mechanically.

### Does the coating provide electrical insulation?

No insulation performance is inferred from an ordinary coating. Any specified insulating components need their own material and whole-equipment verification arrangements.
