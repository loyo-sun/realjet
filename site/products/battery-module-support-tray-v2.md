<h2 id="applications">Where the battery module support tray fits</h2>

### External support within an energy-storage cabinet

Order a custom battery module support tray for a BESS cabinet or energy-storage rack. REALJET cuts, forms and reinforces the external tray to your module-support and cabinet-interface drawings. The permitted contact areas, insertion direction and retention arrangement set its geometry; the module enclosure and live components are separate.

### Insertion, retention and replacement

A tray must leave clearance for the module to enter without scraping its casing or specified insulating pads. Identify retention stops and how they are released during service. Support and restraint requirements follow the actual module and cabinet design.

The external module-support tray and approved metal connections. Battery module casings, internal cold plates, cells and live components are separate; insulation pads or slide strips must be itemised.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Develop the bends around the module path

Laser-cut the outline and fixing features, then bend the side beams and support flanges. Review tool access and springback before setting the tray opening; the route uses formed and joined parts rather than an assumed deep-drawn shell.

### Add reinforcement without closing the clearance

Join the approved stiffeners using a sequence appropriate to the sheet geometry. Check twist and the module support plane after joining. Any change from welded to bolted construction requires the equipment designer’s approval.

### Finish the contact and retention areas

Deburr module-contact edges and finish the critical holes. Follow the approved surface system, then fit any specified pads, strips or retention parts. Trial-fit an unpowered module dummy with the actual support arrangement.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

<h2 id="inspection">Agree the first-article inspection plan</h2>

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insertion geometry | Tray height, side-beam relationship, module clearance and cabinet fixing holes | Module dummy and cabinet-template fit |
| Loaded tray | Deflection and permanent set under the specified load distribution/restraints | Agreed load-test result |
| Contact and retention | Edges, pad identity/thickness, stops and release path | Contact/retention checklist; electrical safety verified separately |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Is the tray the battery module enclosure?

No. It is the external support fitted in the cabinet or rack. A sealed casing, cold plate or live module is not included.

### Can you use the load rating of a similar standard shelf?

No. A custom tray needs its own agreed material, supports, restraint and load verification. A reference shelf rating is not transferred to this design.
