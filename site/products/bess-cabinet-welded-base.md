<h2 id="applications">Where the bess cabinet welded base fits</h2>

### The mechanical interface below an energy-storage cabinet

Source a custom welded cabinet base for a battery energy storage system (BESS), made to your approved cabinet and foundation drawings. REALJET manufactures the mechanical support and specified fixing or handling features. Cabinet footprint, cable access and the installation support plane define the weldment.

### Handling and outdoor installation details

Where the design includes fork pockets, show the required clear opening and the approved handling loads. Cable passages, drainage details and corrosion protection must be coordinated with the cabinet installation rather than added after coating.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Prepare support and handling features

Cut and form the base members, corner supports and reinforcement from the approved arrangement. Check cable and drainage openings, fork access and access for coating inside partially closed sections.

### Weld and check the cabinet mating face

Locate the feet and cabinet support zones, then weld in the agreed sequence. Measure distortion before machining any specified mating faces or anchor features. Trial-fit split parts before finish.

### Protect the installation interfaces

Agree protection for enclosed or hard-to-coat areas. Mask threads and specified bonding contacts, finish to the approved system and recheck openings for coating buildup or blocked drainage.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Does this base give the cabinet an IP or fire rating?

No. Those properties depend on the complete enclosure and system. The base is manufactured and checked to its agreed mechanical and finish requirements.

### Can you include forklift pockets?

Yes, to an approved design defining pocket geometry, load distribution and handling conditions. Their presence alone is not a verified lifting or transport rating.
