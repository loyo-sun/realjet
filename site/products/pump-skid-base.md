<h2 id="applications">Where the machined pump skid base fits</h2>

### Prefabricated data-center cooling pump sets

Order a custom pump skid base for data-center cooling equipment, built to your approved pump and motor drawings. REALJET fabricates the weldment and machines specified seating faces before your team installs the pump set. Your equipment layout defines its support points and mounting relationships.

### Pump and motor interfaces in one datum system

Coupled pump and motor arrangements need their mounting faces related to the same datums. Identify separate pump feet, motor feet and any isolators. Pipe-support locations and access to coupling guards affect the crossmember layout; show them before the skid is welded.

<h2 id="supply">Specify the part and delivery condition</h2>

The fabricated skid, pump and motor seating pads, and drawing-defined pipe-support attachments. Pumps, motors, isolators, pipework and site alignment are separate unless agreed.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Build around the seating arrangement

Cut the members and mounting plates, then locate the pump and motor pads in the welding fixture. Check diagonals and pad allowance at tack-up. Welding order follows the joint design and access; any lifting details must come from the approved drawing.

### Leave final seating work until after welding

Measure distortion before selecting the final machining setup. Where stability requires it, agree stress relief and rough/finish machining stages before manufacture. Mill the specified pump, motor and isolator contacts from common datums, then machine the fixing features.

### Keep the installation condition visible

Mask seats and threads during the agreed finish. Identify split sections and trial-fit the approved joints before packing. State which supports carry the base during inspection and shipment.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

<h2 id="inspection">Agree the first-article inspection plan</h2>

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pump and motor seats | Relative heights, coplanarity and datum-based hole pattern | Seat results with support condition and setup |
| Floor and transport connections | Foot contact, anchor holes, splice fit and any specified lifting joints | Dimensional and joint inspection records |
| Installed pump alignment | Interface gauge for a bare base; soft-foot, alignment and vibration checks after pump fitting if separately ordered | Separate interface and commissioned-equipment results |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Does a bare skid order include pump alignment?

The bare skid is checked against its mounting interfaces. Soft-foot correction, coupling alignment and running vibration checks require the fitted equipment and a separately agreed scope.

### Can the skid be split for transport?

Provide an approved split-joint design and reassembly datums. The quotation can include trial assembly and match marking; the shipping split must not be improvised after fabrication.
