<h2 id="applications">Where the battery module assembly machine frame fits</h2>

### Module assembly and handling stations

Source a custom welded machine frame for battery module assembly equipment, built to your station drawings. REALJET manufactures the conveyor supports, locating interfaces and specified actuator mounting features. Transfer height, tooling-change space and the approved load arrangement determine the frame layout.

### Stations with tightening or pressing equipment

Where the station includes an actuator, supply the load direction and support conditions with its mounting drawing. The resulting reinforcement and seat arrangement are manufactured to the approved design; a frame made for handling is not automatically suitable for a pressing operation.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Separate the load paths in the drawing review

Identify conveyor supports, fixture datums and actuator loads in the assembly view. Cut and form frame parts and reinforcement, then position them from the station datums rather than isolated member dimensions.

### Weld before finishing the locating features

Use the agreed fixture and weld sequence to control twist. Check the weldment and assess stability treatment and rough/finish machining order before machining actuator seats, conveyor interfaces, dowel bores and threads.

### Trial-assemble and clean the station frame

Trial-fit split joints and mark their positions before finish. Remove welding debris and machining chips, protect defined seats during coating and recheck the installation interfaces afterward. Process-specific cleanliness requirements are agreed separately.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

<h2 id="inspection">Agree the first-article inspection plan</h2>

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conveyor and fixture interfaces | Station height, guide relationships, dowel locations and fixing holes | First-article report with support condition |
| Reassembled sections | Locating and connection interfaces after disassembly | Trial-assembly and match-mark record |
| Force and cleanliness requirements | Approved load direction/condition and specified debris or material restrictions | Separate structural or cleanliness verification where required |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Can the frame support a press or robot?

Provide the equipment loads, directions and connection details. The customer-approved design and verification plan must cover that duty before it is included.

### Is every battery-equipment frame dry-room compatible?

No general compatibility is assumed. State the material restrictions, cleanliness, coating and packing requirements for the particular manufacturing process.
