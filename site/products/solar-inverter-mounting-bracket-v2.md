<h2 id="applications">Where the solar inverter mounting bracket fits</h2>

### A mounting interface for a specified inverter

Order a custom steel mounting bracket for a specified solar photovoltaic (PV) inverter and its approved wall, post or equipment support. REALJET cuts, bends and joins the drawing-defined support and retention features. The authorised rear interface, mounting direction and service clearance determine the bracket geometry.

### Different supports need different interfaces

A wall plate and a post adapter do not share the same fixing arrangement. Identify the installation surface, load direction, centre of gravity and approved anchor arrangement. Keep the inverter’s required ventilation and removal space clear of added flanges or gussets.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Form the supporting features from the interface drawing

Cut the fixing slots and outline, then bend the support lips and reinforcing flanges. Confirm hook and lock geometry against the authorised equipment interface rather than a photograph.

### Join and finish the specified reinforcements

Weld any drawing-defined gussets or adapter plates, controlling the bend and mating relationship. Finish locking holes, remove burrs and check fastener access before surface treatment.

### Protect and trial-fit the finished bracket

Agree the environmental coating or external plating route. Recheck slot openings, engagement and locking clearances after finish using the specified inverter or an approved mechanical gauge.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

<h2 id="inspection">Agree the first-article inspection plan</h2>

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hanging and fixing geometry | Slot positions, bend angles, support-lip engagement and rear interface | Authorised mating gauge or equipment fit |
| Retention and loads | Approved weight, centre of gravity, install direction and external loads | Agreed structural/anti-lift checks; anchors reviewed separately |
| Outdoor contacts | Welds, coating and specified dissimilar-material interfaces | Finish record and environmental verification if required |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Can a custom bracket replace any original mounting bracket?

No. Confirm the equipment manufacturer’s installation restrictions and authorisation to use a custom interface. No branded-model compatibility is implied.

### Who selects the wall anchors?

The installation or structural designer defines suitable anchors and the capacity of the actual substrate. Bracket fabrication alone does not verify the wall or post connection.
