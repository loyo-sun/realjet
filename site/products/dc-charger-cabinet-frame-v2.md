<h2 id="applications">Where the dc charger cabinet frame fits</h2>

### The internal structure of a floor-standing DC charger

Source a custom fabricated cabinet frame for floor-standing DC EV charging equipment. REALJET manufactures the open steel structure, module mounting rails and specified panel or duct attachments from your drawings. Module insertion and service access define the layout; power conversion and enclosure performance require the complete equipment.

### Panel and duct interfaces around replaceable modules

Show the module removal route together with door, panel and duct drawings. Folded uprights and crossmembers need to leave tool and cable clearances. If the project expands into a complete enclosure, sealing and environmental tests become a separate agreed scope.

<h2 id="supply">Specify the part and delivery condition</h2>

The mechanical cabinet skeleton and specified mounting rails. Doors, seals, exterior panels, power modules, cables and complete weatherproof or electrical performance are separate unless specifically agreed.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Cut and bend separate frame members

Prepare uprights, rails and attachment plates by laser cutting and bending. Review the actual bend geometry and joining access. The route does not assume a one-piece stamped cabinet profile.

### Join the approved structure

Locate the frame square and join by the specified welding or bolted arrangement. Rivet nuts, clinch fasteners or studs require confirmed equipment, fasteners and connection checks before acceptance into the route.

### Check the mechanical layout after finish

Machine any defined fixing features, deburr cable-contact edges and mask designated conductive contacts during coating. Trial-fit module, panel and duct templates to check their relationships and service access.

REALJET combines plate cutting, bending, welded fabrication and specified milling/boring operations for approved equipment drawings. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

<h2 id="inspection">Agree the first-article inspection plan</h2>

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame and module rails | Squareness, module fixing positions and insertion clearances | Module-template and dimensional report |
| Panel/duct and joints | Attachment locations, fasteners, welds and edge condition | Mechanical fit and connection checklist |
| Complete enclosure requirements | Ingress, thermal, insulation and EMC tests only with the relevant panels/electrical configuration | Separate whole-equipment validation plan |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Is the bare frame waterproof?

An open skeleton has no complete enclosure ingress rating. Panels, seals, cable entries and the assembled configuration need their own approved design and test.

### Can you supply clinched or riveted joints?

Send the specified fastener and joint details. Equipment suitability, access and connection verification must be confirmed before that joining method is quoted.
