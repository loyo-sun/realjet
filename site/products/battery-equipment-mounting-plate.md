<h2 id="applications">Where the machined battery equipment mounting plate fits</h2>

### An interface plate inside battery assembly equipment

Order a custom machined steel mounting plate for battery manufacturing or assembly equipment. REALJET machines the specified fixture face, locating bores, guide or actuator fixings from your approved datum scheme. The plate joins the station structure to its mounted equipment; it is not a battery enclosure or cold plate.

### Replaceable fixture and guide assemblies

For a removable fixture or guide module, distinguish locating bores from clearance holes. Tool access, counterbores and the refitting direction determine which plate features need machining and measurement. State whether inspection is in the free state or on defined supports.

<h2 id="process">Manufacturing around your mounting interfaces</h2>

### Choose the blank around the finished features

Review material condition and residual stress before selecting a cut blank or purchased plate. Leave machining allowance for the faces and any thermally affected cut edges that need removal.

### Manage support and machining order

Rough the relevant faces and features using controlled supports. Where needed, agree stabilisation between rough and finish machining. Avoid relying on heavy clamping to create a flat measurement condition that the free plate cannot retain.

### Finish and identify the hole system

Mill the specified faces; drill, ream or bore the locating features and tap the defined threads. Deburr and clean, then follow the drawing-defined finish/masking sequence. Identify inspection features by the drawing hole numbers.

REALJET uses plate preparation, milling, boring and machining centres to review the defined faces and hole features. The quotation confirms the material, usable machine envelope, tooling and inspection setup for your part. A required external operation is identified before manufacture.

| Feature | Check to agree | Record for acceptance |

| Locating bores and threads | Bore size/position and specified thread gauges | Numbered hole report and gauge results |
| Released and finished plate | Free-state recheck where specified, surface roughness and mating fit | Separate post-release/finish records; appropriate instrument provision agreed |

Mark the drawing revision, datums, inspection limits and measurement support condition before the sample is made. The measuring method and available instruments must suit the feature and tolerance; confirm any specialist measurement or test provision in the quotation.

Approve the sample or pilot batch against the agreed results before repeat supply. Record accepted changes in the drawing revision and inspection plan.

## Protect the interfaces during delivery

Agree packing supports, protection for machined contacts and threads, and identification for any mating sections. Send the quantity, destination and required shipping terms so packing and freight scope can be included in the quote.

## Questions before you order

### Are locating holes treated like bolt clearance holes?

No. Identify each function and fit on the drawing. Locating bores need the specified finishing and size/position checks, while clearance holes follow their own requirements.

### Can you quote a tight flatness or roughness requirement?

Send the value, datum and measurement condition. The machining setup and suitable measuring instruments must be confirmed before that requirement is accepted.
