## Implementation

- Unique search-oriented title, summary and H1 on every page.
- Added 32 equipment-specific selection questions and answers; retained original configuration and RFQ inputs.
- Three explicitly selected related products per equipment page; no self-links.
- Related utility/scope guides linked from the product content.
- Existing WebPage and BreadcrumbList structured data retained; no invented offers, reviews or FAQ rich-result claims.
- Existing enquiry/WhatsApp conversion tracking retained.
- No fabricated ratings, throughput, dimensions, compliance marks, delivery claims or customer cases.
- Images retained unchanged and displayed with object-contain.

## Measurement handoff

After deployment: inspect indexing for all 16 URLs in Search Console when access is available. At 2, 4 and 8 weeks, compare relevant impressions, clicks, query intent and product enquiries. These are review intervals, not ranking promises. No recurring automation was created.
